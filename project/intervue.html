Project Overview
Frontend
The Intervue platform is a user-friendly web application designed to facilitate mock interviews for job seekers. Built using HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript, this frontend solution connects users with industry professionals who provide practice interviews and constructive feedback. The platform aims to enhance the interview skills of job seekers by simulating real-world interview scenarios, allowing them to gain confidence and improve their performance. With an intuitive interface and seamless user experience, Intervue serves as a valuable resource for individuals preparing for job interviews across various industries.
